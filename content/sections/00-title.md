## Yikes

### Yikes! What's with all the tooling and fluff?

For better or worse, I figured it'd be preferable to represent myself at my default settings at this particular moment
in time than guess the ergonomic preferences of my reader. While I may have taken more liberties than wise, I do hope
showing "if left to her own devices, these are some of Jess's devices" is communicative.

As such, my use of justfile entrypoint, nix2container, nix flake, the pattern of working from properties and mappable
assertions to derive a specification in Dhall and QuickCheck are not to be taken as "Jess thinks these are actually the
most minimal, efficient or best tools for the job" but "this is Jess in her natural habitat".
